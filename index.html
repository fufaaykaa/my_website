My Journey to Game Design

I am a Bachelor's Graduate in Game Design. My journey into Game Design began almost 4 years ago. Before that, I had been a professional dancer for 12 years. Over time, I became fascinated by the IT world, which led me to explore programming, mathematics, and other technical fields. However, learning informatics was not enough for me, because I wanted to find a way to merge my technical skills with creativity. For my whole life, I've been drawn to different forms of art: I play piano, sing and record covers, love creative makeup, I am interested in painting and poetry, and also have a big background in the dancing industry. Therefore, when I learned about Game Design, it seemed like the perfect field for me, since it combines both technical skills and artistic expression. Every day I feel more confident about the choice I've made. There is still a lot to explore and learn about game development and my interests as a game creator, but I already know that I want to grow in this industry and share my ideas with people through the art of games.
